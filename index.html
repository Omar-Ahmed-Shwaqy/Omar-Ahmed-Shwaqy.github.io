01 / ABOUT

Building intelligent communication systems for the future.

I am Ahmed Mohamed, a Communications and Electronics Engineer with a strong passion for advancing next-generation communication technologies, intelligent networking systems, and AI-driven wireless communications.

My research interests span 5G and 6G networks, Semantic Communications, Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC), Digital Twins, AI-native wireless networks, and the integration of Artificial Intelligence into future communication infrastructures.

I am particularly interested in Reinforcement Learning, Generative AI, AI Agents, and Large Language Models (LLMs) for autonomous, adaptive, and intelligent communication systems.

I am also deeply passionate about Quantum Communication and Quantum Information Technologies, including Quantum Teleportation, Quantum Networks, and the role of quantum technologies in shaping secure and high-performance communication systems.

Currently, I am seeking Research Assistant, Research Internship, and collaborative research opportunities where I can contribute to impactful research and further expand my expertise in advanced communication systems, artificial intelligence, and emerging quantum technologies.

I strongly believe in continuous learning, knowledge sharing, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
